Mercury Works

An autonomous AI creative agency. One sentence in, a complete live brand out, with nobody touching it in between.

Built withPiyush Rane
WhereWorld's Largest Hermes Buildathon, GrowthX, Bengaluru, 12 July 2026
Result11 real live brand sites shipped inside 8 hours
Codegithub.com/SuryanshD/mercury-works · MIT

The idea

Type one sentence describing a brand you want to exist. Get back a brand that exists: named, designed, illustrated, with a radio ad you can listen to and a website you can buy from, live at a URL.

The constraint we set was the hard part. Zero human edits, including the tidy-up pass. If the chain produces something broken, the output stays broken.

Why zero edits is hard

A generative pipeline usually gets away with loose output because a human is standing at the end tidying up. With nobody there, each stage's output has to be usable by the next stage exactly as emitted. The naming stage has to produce a name the design stage can lay out. The design stage has to produce values the deploy stage can put in a stylesheet.

Errors compound too. A weak positioning line early on becomes a whole brand built on it, because every downstream stage takes it as truth.

The result we actually care about

Eleven real brand sites, live, inside an eight-hour buildathon. Eleven separate briefs is enough to show the chain holds together on inputs it had not seen.

The buildathon was hosted by GrowthX in Bengaluru and sponsored by OpenAI, Cloudflare, Convex, ElevenLabs and Razorpay. The repository is MIT-licensed and the exact stack is in it, which is a better source for implementation details than a paragraph of prose here.

Building it as a chain of separate stages

Separate stages with defined outputs, rather than asking one model to produce a whole brand in a single call.

cost More orchestration and more places to fail. In exchange you can see which stage produced a bad result, which you cannot do inside a single call.

Ending at a deployed URL rather than a downloadable bundle

The output is a live site with working checkout.

cost Deployment becomes part of the pipeline's failure surface, so a deploy failure looks like a product failure.
What eight hours cost us

There's no evaluation of output quality. We know the chain completes and that eleven brands came out of it, which tells you nothing about how good they are. The next thing would be scoring the outputs against a rubric across many briefs. Right now all we know is that it ran.
